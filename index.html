FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the calculator free?

Yes. The core calculator is free and does not require an account.

Does FollowUp Engine replace a CRM?

No. It is intentionally focused on follow-up prioritization rather than trying to replace a full CRM.

Who is FollowUp Engine for?

It is designed for freelancers, consultants, agencies, salespeople, coaches and small service businesses managing sales opportunities.

Is my calculator data uploaded?

No. Your calculator inputs and score are calculated locally in your browser. Optional feedback is submitted separately when you choose to provide it.

What is FollowUp Engine Pro?

Pro is a planned one-time $19 product designed to expand the free calculator into a more complete follow-up workflow.

Is Pro a monthly subscription?

No. The current plan is a one-time purchase of $19. The product is currently in the validation and pre-launch stage.

How do I join the Pro early access list?

Click the early access button, enter your email in the form, and you will be notified when FollowUp Engine Pro becomes available.
